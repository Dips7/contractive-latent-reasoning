## 2. Hard Epistemic Distinctions to Maintain Throughout Package

1. **Exact Resolvent Identity vs. Sensitivity Inequality**:  
   - The algebraic Neumann series identity $(I_{nd} - \mathcal{T})^{-1} = \sum_{m=0}^\infty \mathcal{T}^m$ is an exact algebraic representation of the linearized equilibrium system under the convergence condition $\|\mathcal{T}\|_2 \le \rho < 1.0$.
   - The resulting sensitivity formula $\|\partial z^*_v / \partial x_s\|_2 \le \frac{1}{\alpha} \rho^k$ is an **analytical upper bound** derived from taking operator norms across shortest-path nilpotency, not an equality or tight bound.

2. **Empirical Test Loss vs. Classification Performance**:  
   - Held-out test cross-entropy loss evaluates continuous probabilistic prediction confidence. Candidate B achieves 47.7% to 69.3% lower loss.
   - Classification accuracy, specificity, and recall are at 100.0% ceiling across all models (contractive, undamped, and discrete GNN6) on these small digraphs under multi-instance training. The benchmark does not discriminate models by classification recall.

3. **Descriptive Parameter Association vs. Causal Attribution**:  
   - The damping sweep evaluates empirical associations across nominal damping settings under fixed hyperparameters and PRNG seeds.
   - It does not isolate confounders or establish a causal mechanism between coordinate damping and loss or recall.
