# Research Highlights

**Manuscript Title**: Stability and Long-Range Information Propagation in Continuous-Time Graph Neural Dynamics: Sensitivity Analysis, Audited Benchmarks, and Certified Multiscale Architectures  
**Target Journal**: *Neurocomputing* (Elsevier)

- Derived spatial sensitivity upper bound for contractive graph neural ODEs
- Shortest-path nilpotency bounds equilibrium sensitivity decay to (rho)^k
- Small synthetic benchmarks show 100% multi-hop reachability recall at ceiling
- Multiscale skip-hop ODE cuts held-out test loss by up to 69% with stability
